# AGI Pro Labs

Research and engineering in forecasting, prediction markets and decentralized AI networks.

## Data attribution

### Weather forecasts on Bittensor Subnet 18 (Zeus)

Our Subnet 18 miner publishes weather forecasts produced by our own blending and post-processing system from several
numerical and machine-learning weather models.

**Google DeepMind WeatherNext 3.** Our forecasts use WeatherNext 3 ensemble statistics, accessed through Google Cloud
Storage, as one input among several. The WeatherNext 3 data is combined with other models and modified by our own
learned corrections; the published forecasts are our own derived product.

© 2024-6 Google LLC, whose machine learning models were used to create the experimental data made available under the
following licence terms https://storage.googleapis.com/weathernext-public/terms-of-use.pdf. This data is intended for
experimental modelling only and is not intended, validated, or approved for real world use.

Our forecasts are not endorsed by Google or by any meteorological agency, and are not official weather warnings.

**Other sources.** ECMWF open data (IFS and AIFS forecasts, CC BY 4.0) and the Copernicus ERA5 reanalysis (Copernicus
Climate Change Service).
